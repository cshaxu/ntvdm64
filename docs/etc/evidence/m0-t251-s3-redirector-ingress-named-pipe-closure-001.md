# M0 T251 S3 — Redirector ingress and named-pipe owner group closure

## Scope and source boundary

OpenNT retains `rdrsvc.h`, the 16-bit `redir/namepipe.asm` clients and the
`VDMREDIR` ABI headers, but not the `VrDispatch` provider bodies.  S3 is thus
a source-derived `bx-vdm` composition seam, not a copied VDMREDIR DLL and not
a Bochs change.

## Delivered route

- `C4 C4 57 <service>` has one typed, selector-57 adapter ingress.
- `57:00` and `57:01` own one session-local Redirector lifecycle and resume
  at `RIP+4` with the original success carry convention.
- `57:29/2A` retain the bounded session mode state.
- The admitted-but-not-yet-recovered pipe protocol services `02..08,20/21`
  resume with CF set and `ERROR_INVALID_FUNCTION`; no copied register is
  treated as a guest pointer and no success is fabricated.
- `LoadVdmRedir`, `VrConvertLocalNtPipeName`, `VrAdd/RemoveOpenNamedPipeInfo`,
  `VrIsNamedPipeHandle`, `VrReadNamedPipe`, and `VrWriteNamedPipe` are no
  longer DEM-local placeholders.  They forward to the same session provider.
  Native handles remain private to the existing opaque handle manager.

## Verification

`build/M0-T251-S3/formal-r3` was generated by the formal MSVC x64 `/MT`
Ninja graph outside the sandbox.  Its focused target
`t251-s3-redir-ingress-fixture.exe` passed, proving lifecycle, unavailable
service result, and bidirectional public-Win32 named-pipe read/write through
the shared DEM helper seam.

The broader formal-r1 all-target link remains blocked by pre-existing fixture
membership: several unrelated fixture link lines omit the existing
`opennt_int06_provider_v1_*` owner.  The new provider itself compiled and the
isolated formal-r3 target links with its declared dependencies.

## Deferred owner groups

Async pipes (`23/24`), mailslots (`09..0F`), NetAPI/identity (`10..1F,22,
2B..2E`), and NetBIOS/DLC/VDM-window (`25..28,2F..31`) remain separate
Redirector owner groups.  They must preserve their OpenNT packed structures
and session/PDB ownership; S3 does not claim their protocol semantics.
