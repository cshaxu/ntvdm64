   frontier, linkage and original variant are compatible.  A selected body may
   be read solely to emit direct second-degree call identities.
4. Keep source-absent, macro/assembly/indirect and multiple-variant outcomes
   explicit.  They are not silently merged by spelling or guessed as a modern
   provider.

No source import, adapter implementation, link/build change, BOP enablement
or second-degree body inspection is in scope.
