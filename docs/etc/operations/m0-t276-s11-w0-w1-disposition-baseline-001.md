# M0 T276 S11: W0/W1 file and interface disposition baseline

## Scope and method

This is the first package-level disposition slice of T276.  It covers exactly
the six W0/W1 roots selected by S10: `dirs`, `makefil0`, `inc`, `oemuni`,
`suballoc`, and `vdmutils`.  It is an audit result, not an enabled build graph
or an adapter implementation.

The result is regenerated by
`tools/governance/Audit-T276S11W0W1Disposition.ps1` from the canonical T276
file, build, and lexical-call ledgers.  The companion verifier proves the
expected root/file/family/call coverage and rejects any enabled state.

## Exact accounting

- 140 selected mirror paths are assigned one provisional composition
  disposition and final component owner: 112 support-owned paths (one `dirs`
  build-control input, one `makefil0` build-control input, 96 `inc`
  declaration/resource carriers, six `oemuni` paths and eight `suballoc`
  paths), plus 28 tool-owned `vdmutils` paths.
- 12 source translation units are explicitly represented by 14
  source-interface-family rows.  The split is deliberate: `oemuni/file.c`
  and `oemuni/process.c` each cross both public Win32 and historical
  NTDLL/RTL-facing surfaces.
- All 2,270 textual call candidates under these roots are preserved in the
  call-accounting ledger.  A lexical call is not promoted to an ABI, provider,
  linkability, or adapter claim merely by appearing in C source.

## Composition result

- `dirs`, `makefil0`, and `inc` are original source/build carriers.  They
  supply no standalone runtime object in this profile.  Their headers remain
  available to later owner-package work without inventing an `inc` library.
- `oemuni/file.c` and `oemuni/process.c` are the two original library
  translation units.  Their algorithm remains mirror-owned.  They require a
  later same-shaped `adapter-win32` binding review for public Win32 and NT
  RTL/PEB/TEB conversion/heap surfaces; this audit does not change either
  source body or include.
- `oemuni/toemuni.c` is the original `UMTEST`, not an `oemuni.lib` member.
  It is profile-excluded rather than silently compiled as proof of the library.
- `suballoc/suballoc.c` is the sole original library translation unit.  Its
  CRT allocation and optional diagnostic surface remain an x86/x64 binding
  question; no custom allocator is introduced by this packet.
- `suballoc/tsa.c` is the original allocator stress `UMTEST`; `debug.c` is
  not in the literal original library/test source set.  Both are explicitly
  profile-excluded, preserving their exact mirrors as evidence.
- `vdmutils/forcedos`, `graftabl`, `pifedit`, and `win` are original standalone
  console/Windows products, not reusable MVDM host-runtime libraries.
  `pifedit` is specifically a GUI/shell/common-dialog product.  Their sources
  and support inputs are retained exactly but profile-excluded; none becomes a
  hidden provider or justification for an adapter.

## Interface and wave handoff

The W0/W1 result has no `exact-direct` or `exact-adapter-bound` final
classification yet.  That is intentional: final classes require an admitted
formal x86/x64 target and exact adapter ABI/link evidence, which S11 is
forbidden to enable.

The only candidate runtime source bodies handed forward are the original
`oemuni` library pair and `suballoc/suballoc.c`. Their next prerequisite is the
three-row runtime-support binding ledger. `oemuni/file.c` and `process.c`
require an interface-by-interface `adapter-win32` review, retaining original
names, parameters, error conversion, allocation and temporary-string ownership
semantics. `suballoc.c` remains an original algorithm library with `ULONG`
address values and caller-supplied callbacks; only its callers may later bind
those callbacks through `adapter-softpc`. This handoff does not authorize a
source modification or a generic compatibility layer.

All `vdmutils` programs remain a later product-profile decision.  They are
not predecessors of the MVDM host package waves, so their current exclusion
cannot block a runtime provider from being recovered.
