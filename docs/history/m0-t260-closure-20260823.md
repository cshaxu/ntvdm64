# M0 T260 closure — eight-component production layout

## Outcome

T260 closes the non-behavioural eight-component source-layout migration.  The
live `src/` tree has exactly these production components:

`bx-core`, `bx-mantle`, `opennt-guest`, `opennt-host`, `opennt-bop`,
`adapter-win32`, `adapter-softpc`, and `app`.

`opennt-guest` remains the artifact-only eighth component.  The other seven
are the formal static-library inputs.  No legacy `src/opennt`, `src/bx-vdm`,
or `src/cli` root remains.

## Production-tree result

- Compiled OpenNT BOP and host bodies now sit in their original owner-family
  groups, rather than generic `original`, `mirror`, `overlay` or `capability`
  staging trees.
- Reference-only originals, obsolete overlays, uncompiled fragments and
  architecture-excluded headers now reside under `docs/etc/legacy_code/`;
  fixtures reside under `tests/`.
- Every retained nonzero adapted production expression is source marked and
  registered: 31 `BOP-DIV`, 11 `HOST-DIV`, two `SOFTPC-DIV`, and two
  `WIN32-DIV` entries.  The adopted-Bochs exception register remains the
  separate authority for `bx-core` changes; `opennt-guest` has no source edit.

## Boundary

T260 changes ownership, paths, build inputs and evidence only.  It neither
enables a new BOP/provider nor changes Bochs, guest or host capability
semantics.  The next product packet requires explicit owner admission from
the ordered candidate queue.
