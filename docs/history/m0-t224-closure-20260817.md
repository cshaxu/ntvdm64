ran under MSVC x64 `/MT` in disposable
`build/M0-T224-S5/012-package-route-profiles/` with `linkExitCode: 0`,

## Retained Limitations And Transfer

The historical `cmdexec.c` broker/event-thread and `cmdredir.c` pipe worker
composition cannot directly link into the MSVC x64 CLI process because of its
CCPU/SAS, 32-bit pointer, SoftPC and legacy-thread dependencies. The retained
source-derived seam deliberately does not claim pipe creation, `cmdPipeList`,
DEM `50:47/48` success, ambient process launching, or a native guest trace.
Those remain separate whole-owner packages. The Queue-selected successor is
Guest DOS multi-program execution closure; its first S is a source/ABI/gate
audit before any EXEC or trace implementation.
