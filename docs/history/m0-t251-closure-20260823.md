## Explicit transfer

T251 does **not** claim remote/network Redirector, NetAPI, NetBIOS/DLC,
VDM-window, or functional async named-pipe completion.  The async dependency
chain is a future cross-owner task: original guest SFT state must publish only
an opaque token, and a selector-blind machine seam must deliver `int5c.asm`'s
guest continuation before bx-vdm can restore the original serialized queue.
It may not be bypassed with a raw `HANDLE`, guest callback, CCPU worker, CSR,
or BaseSrv recreation.
