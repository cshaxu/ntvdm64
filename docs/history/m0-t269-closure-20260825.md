# M0 T269 Closure — OpenNT-host Mirror and Overlay Convergence

## Closed outcome

T269 makes `src/opennt-host` a compliant OpenNT mirror component. Its ten
production units now have a selected original baseline and one valid
classification: six are byte-identical, four are locally marked/registered
subsets or same-shaped modifications, and all private binding code is confined
to `src/opennt-host-overlay`.

## Key boundary repair

The sole direct cross-component body inclusion was the exact OpenNT
`nt_pif.c` parser. Because its only reached user is the COMMAND PIF
translation unit and its own historical `PIF_DATA` conflicts with COMMAND's
layout, the source was transferred byte-identically to its actual
`opennt-bop` composition owner. No new transport ABI or parser rewrite was
introduced.

## Verification and limitation

The final T269 Ninja graph is incrementally clean. Its direct PIF fixture and
focused host-mirror fixture passed. The task does not claim recovery of the
unadmitted async Redirector/VDD product shell or the broader COMMAND lifecycle
fixture that did not progress during S3.
