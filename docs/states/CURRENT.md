project-authored substitute against its selected original x86 source. Remove
the mapping manager, its session instances and unneeded token paths only where
the original x86 pointer/HANDLE carrier is directly composable. Replace or
remove a project-authored substitute unless an explicit source-first record
shows it is the smallest required x86 seam. Preserve a bounded guest-memory
lease/copy mechanism only where the selected x86 graph independently requires
it. Then build, link, verify and stage the sole Win32/x86 product.
