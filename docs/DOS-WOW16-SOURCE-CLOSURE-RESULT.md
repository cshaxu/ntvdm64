## WOW16 distinction

`MOUSE.DRV`, `VGA.DRV`, `KEYBOARD.DRV`, `COMM.DRV`, and `SOUND.DRV` are final driver outputs with retained historical MASM/LINK16/RC16 evidence. They do not close the Win16 runtime. The ledger separately enumerates `kernel31`, `user`, `gdi`, `commdlg`, `ddeml`, `mciole`, `mmsystem`, `ole`, `shell`, `system`, `toolhelp`, `winsock`, `winspool`, `write`, and the other full-reference WOW16 descriptor sets. Where the selected fixed source island lacks their original source directory, the result is `source-blocked`, not a substituted modern build.

## Non-DOS/WOW16 guest-side result

The full-reference descriptors also expose DPMI, DPMI32, SIM/V86 monitor/scaffold, redirector, and related candidate paths. `DOSX.EXE` is already accounted for by the final `source-built` DOS record. The remaining logical targets are recorded as `host/support` or `sample/development-only` with their first composition/profile blocker. No target with only a historical object or `TARGETNAME` declaration is presented as a built guest artifact.
