# 6.3 Sound, Removable Media, and VDD

## Boundary

6.3 covers the host interfaces reached by DOS sound, floppy/CD/removable-media
routes and VDD loading/callback use. It does not promote sample VDDs, guest
drivers, or all device internals into product scope. The module's fixed ledger
boundary is statically accounted for; concrete provider, exported-object ABI
and callback/runtime validation remain open implementation work.

## Semantics to retain

Dynamic loading, symbol lookup, device/media identity, callback registration,
notification ordering, native resource ownership, close/cancel and late
delivery each require independent evidence. A declared export does not prove an
actual image export; successful loading does not prove the callback ABI; a NULL
or unregister-style notification does not itself prove that callbacks have
stopped. Removable-media access must not broaden host privilege or expose raw
devices simply to imitate an old name.

## Minimum-diff direction

Retain original call shape, registration/retirement sequence and guest-visible
status. Bind a finite host service only after its scope and lifetime are known;
do not replace it with a fresh sound/media/VDD framework. Storage semantics
remain 3.4, timing/cancellation 6.1 and 2.3, and selected provider/object/ABI/
runtime acceptance Part 8.
