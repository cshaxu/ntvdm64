The independently controlled [CCPU bit-fault non-pass](s11-foundation-evidence/current-ccpu-execution-fixture/review.md)
remains visible: case 0 expects a valid negative-index access but the current
original CCPU faults. The owner explicitly excluded independent internal CPU
repairs absent a demonstrated in-scope connection or real workload failure.
This is not one of the 16 recorded passing Full gates; neither that Full
receipt nor this scope decision proves the separate test passes. Do not change
its assertion or report universal test success. Reassess only if an in-scope
connection or real guest manifestation is established, or the owner changes
that boundary.

The historical [Winmine 1400 non-pass](s11-foundation-evidence/winmine-native-return-1400/review.md)
is another unlocalized native-return observation. Its failure substage and
window are unknown; the separately captured and repaired cross-thread root
retirement race cannot be assumed to explain it or the historical SOL failure.
The [hook graph lock boundary](s11-foundation-evidence/hook-graph-lock-boundary.md)
has a controlled overlap witness and a published serialization repair; that
witness does not attribute either historical failure.
Four additional Winmine dialog interactions passed under a separately staged
identity-diagnostic provider; the [receipt](s11-foundation-evidence/winmine-return-identity-current/review.md)
records its minimized-window and diagnostic-coverage limitations.
The same diagnostic provider also passed a [shared-worker idle/dialog
witness](s11-foundation-evidence/current-identity-shared-idle-dialog/review.md);
this does not force the historic race.

S11 cannot be declared fully complete on this checkpoint: the observed SOL
failure has not been attributed, and the existing installation is not a
complete manifest-verified guest environment. The remaining work is bounded
by those specific facts and the recorded owner handoffs; repeated successful
SOL runs, more unforced checks, or generic claims that a foundation is
incomplete are not substitutes for evidence. No new compatibility concession
or guest modification is authorized here.
