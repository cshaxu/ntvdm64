# Fifteen pending headers: exact-file disposition

Coordinator follow-up: `gfiempty.h`, `keycaps.h` and `nt_paint.h` were subsequently excluded only as physical roles in the inspected product configuration. Their respective GFI, keyboard and paint capabilities remain separately in scope; the remaining recommendations are a snapshot, not current ledger decisions.

This is a per-file static/source and existing-evidence review of the captured CPU40/NTVDM x86 production profile. All 15 files match the corresponding OpenNT source bytes. “Excluded” below means **the identified header role in this specific profile**, not deleting source or excluding the associated guest capability. Existing reports remain the source for full test setup and limitations. No new test or shared-ledger change was made.
