Scope is shard 3 `inc/wowsnd.h`. `sound-abi-observer.py` and its captured JSON reproduce full SHA-256 comparison against both original trees, including related sound sources and existing linker-map excerpts. Proposed disposition: `inc/wowsnd.h` remains **pending**. Header identity and inclusion cannot establish actual runtime thunk table bytes, guest-to-host ABI or the correctness and audibility of sound playback; the observed map is not bound here to the currently published DLL. The source-level unimplemented thunk entries are present in both original editions, but their historical guest call paths, product expectations and any alternate sound route require separate analysis; do not claim that all sound is absent or restored. Shard 2 owns the table file itself. No aggregate behavioral restoration percentage follows from these observations.
