# Guest images and historical build inputs

The companion dispositions reconcile GDI.EXE as partial based on original,
packaged and installed identity, and eight historical guest build-input,
documentation or intermediate file roles as excluded. The latter include
CTL3D reference materials, a GDI resource, and the GRAPHICS linker intermediate
and change log. The separate TOOLHELP observation rechecks fifteen roles
already classified; it does not change their classification.

These decisions do not exclude the GDI, TOOLHELP, CTL3D or GRAPHICS features.
Their guest loading, image lineage, runtime behavior, failure and lifecycle
remain separate obligations. Other unproven CTL3D and GRAPHICS inputs remain
pending. No product or guest image was modified or rebuilt.
