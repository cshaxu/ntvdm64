`wutbl2.h:255-272,308` dispatches original USER COMM ordinals into `wucomm.c`. Original process-local PortTab encodes COM1..9 and LPT1..3. `WU32OpenComm` establishes native device/overlapped resources, state and owning task thread; read/write, state, event and notification routines resolve that port. `WU32CloseComm` uses `DeletePortTabEntry`, whose COM branch marks close, purges, resets comm mask, waits for modem and writer workers, closes events/handle and releases queue and port. `wkman.c::WowOriginalFreeTask` calls `FreeCommSupportResources(dwThreadID)` on task retirement; the original loop breaks after the first matching entry. `m0-t469-s1-comm-owner-matrix.md` documents the contrast with guest-side multi-port cleanup and checked historical upstream source. This remains a documented original-host limit, not newly proven regression.

The unchanged source waits at most 1.5 seconds for modem thread and 5 seconds for writer, then closes thread handles and frees associated state. Source inspection shows the free occurs even when a wait has timed out; a still-running thread could matter, but this review has not demonstrated a live worker, a use-after-free, a production defect, or the original platform contract under those failures. Any future test must use an isolated deterministic blocked-IO harness and establish actual thread access and shutdown before proposing a repair. Do not delete inherited timeout or invent a replacement contract solely from this observation.
