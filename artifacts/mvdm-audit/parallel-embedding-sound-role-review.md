# Embedding and Win16 sound owners

The [embedding source](shard-1/wow-embedding-review.md) is classified partial
after its six functions and registry-binding edges were checked against the
released WOW32 image. The [sound driver image](shard-2/win16-sound-guest-review.md)
is partial after its original, packaged and installed bytes were compared.
The two files match both pinned original editions. Their 100% source/image
byte retention does not measure functional restoration.

Guest execution, runtime sound, profile and registry failures, ownership,
timing and teardown remain open; the bounded embedding failure candidates
and XMS boundaries are retained in the [worklist](recovery-worklist.md).
No guest or product image was modified.
