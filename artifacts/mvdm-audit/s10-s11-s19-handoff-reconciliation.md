The worklist contains considerably more detail than the short stage table; its controlled failures must not be described as proven real guest crashes. A source-file physical exclusion is limited to the stated profile and does not exclude the relevant capability.

## Concrete handoff gaps and corrections recommended before S10 closure

1. **Make the detailed worklist a direct stage-plan handoff.** The stage plan links two reproduced-failure tables and several individual reviews, but does not directly link `recovery-worklist.md`, which carries extensive finite S11–S18 obligations, including the actual-caller FCB evidence, 486 activation group, device lifecycle and other source risks. Add a direct link stating that its named owner groups and evidence limitations survive S10; explicitly separate proven object/fixture defects, conditional risks and untested capabilities. This avoids silently retaining only the short stage-table scope.
2. **Give the remaining SOL Options failure an initial owner and exit condition.** The current plan correctly says it is unclassified and must not be assigned merely from the application; however, it gives no stage or finite acceptance condition. Assign investigation of 5023 `WindowCreate`/borrow state and the separately observed missing-dialog outcome initially to S11 as an execution/identity triage, with S14/S15 taking any proved module-specific defect. Preserve both passing and failing evidence and require original/candidate artifact binding and actual failure witness before declaring any root cause or fix. The non-instrumented four-round pass does not erase the prior failures. This is not a claim the NIDDB candidate caused them.
3. **Record the last 21 host-candidate role handoffs, not just classification counts.** The [machine CSV](s10-remaining-host-final-candidates.csv) records each physical file, observed source role, owner and unverified boundary. S11 owns context/register ABI; S12/S17 selected startup configuration; S13 the actual video/virtual-floppy device contracts; S14 actual DDEML route; S17 HFX, HUNTER, YODA/profile, standalone VDMDBG and legacy UI/provider applicability. If classified `partial`, specify declaration/source-role partial rather than claiming execution. Do not copy `inc/monregs.h` evidence to `softpc.new/host/inc/monregs.h`, or infer identity of same-named UI/platform headers. `nt_vflop.c` is already explicitly carried by worklist S13; HUNTER, standalone VDMDBG and legacy configuration/UI ownership deserve explicit linkable handoffs rather than reliance on catch-all S17.
4. **Carry disjoint guest/shared roles and tooling into the capability worklist.** The [WOW16 role review](s10-followup-wow16-ownership.md), [DOS v86 role review](s10-followup-dos-v86-ownership.md), and [tool/extension review](s10-followup-tool-boundaries.md) show source-role classification, not guest-binary provenance or complete external-client behavior. For shared-header or same-basename cases retain actual preprocessor resolution and ABI checks in S11–S17 according to function; keep immutable guest image load/entry/retirement validation in S12/S14–S17. Standalone `VDMDBG`, debugger extension, PIFEDIT/FORCEDOS/GRAFTABL/WIN applicability and behavior need explicit owners; the worklist already names some but not all. Do not rebuild guest just to establish provenance.
5. **Unify conflicting stage labels at the owner-group level.** The vfloppy review assigns S13; old HUNTER reopening notes S11 while current host follow-up uses S17. Since HUNTER is a diagnostic profile, assign its applicability/provider/teardown to S17, with S11 retaining any CPU-state ABI or callback prerequisite. The utility review suggests S12/S17 broadly, whereas worklist places PIFEDIT and FORCEDOS in S17 with a separate DOS launch/PIF owner in S12; keep that split explicit and avoid counting the same investigation twice.
6. **Bind new selected-object checkpoints and stage exit assertions.** S19 must repeat/rebind each required controlled failure and product regression to the actual coherent package; frozen source/object/map evidence has different identities from a release unless specifically linked. Include normal, negative and teardown scenarios for each recovered group; prove guest path only where a guest claim is made. The candidate Full run's 14 gates and visible gameplay do not establish every separate lifecycle or compatibility edge.

## Minimum decision rules

The later stage may determine an alternative original physical provider is unnecessary only after tracing the observable capability and selecting its authoritative owner; static nonselection alone is insufficient. Historical debugger utilities and device alternatives require an applicability conclusion, not automatic restoration and not automatic deletion. Unreproduced failure paths remain explicitly conditional until exercised at the actual owner. Repairs against imported mirror code require registered exceptions and minimum diff. Guest files and images remain immutable.
