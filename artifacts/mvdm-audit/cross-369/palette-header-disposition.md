Determine the actual helper behavior and output contract for short/zero/error returns, temporary-buffer initialization and guest boundaries; no runtime defect is claimed. Compare the separately implemented `WG32GetSystemPaletteEntries`, which passes the returned count and has an original high-color fallback, without assuming their contracts are interchangeable.
