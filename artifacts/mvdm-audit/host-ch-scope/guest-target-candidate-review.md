These 98 are candidates for **guest source in the observed target/profile only**, not proof of source-to-installed-binary provenance or permission to modify/rebuild guest, and not grounds to exclude the behavior of WRITE, DDEML or COMMDLG from WOW32 host audit. The other **375 rows remain unresolved** here.
