# WOW16 core guest source role — shard 1

The CSV covers 54 currently pending shard-1 files in USER, GDI, SYSTEM, MMSYSTEM and drivers: 36 candidate historical guest inputs and 18 still pending. Every row records its frozen/current SHA and checked OpenNT original hash, exact rule or explicit unresolved state. Candidates trace concrete source/definition/include/resource into original 16-bit MASM/CL16/RC16 and link16 target; they do not prove a deployed image derives from these files or that the corresponding guest behavior passes. The CSV records variant constraints, notably MMSYSTEM `mmsex.c` only in debug `OBJD` and MMTASK resource under !LANG. Locally retained `.RES` and VGA.DRV are not classified as source solely by their suffix.

USER uses OBJ1..4 and optional x86 OBJ386 with generated userk.lrf; its own code, definition and resource compile/link to USER.EXE. GDI uses its source/linked layer and RC to GDI.EXE. SYSTEM links system/timer objects to SYSTEM.DRV. MMSYSTEM OBJ1..5/OBJD links to MMSYSTEM.DLL and has an MMTASK.TSK dependency. Display/mouse and comm use their distinct driver targets. Unclear `user/k/daytona/wowk.asm` and `wowrtl.asm` remain pending because the makefile has generated/copy steps whose byte provenance was not established. Conditional inclusion and localization are historical roles, not assertion about every variant.

Only source role is proposed: no host runtime admission or guest functionality is excluded. The supplemental manifest was checked for this cohort; no unmatched provenance is promoted. Product, guest and main ledger are untouched.
