previously described in the [investigation](../../s11-dialog-performance/investigation.md).

subsequently returned 9. The worker trace ends with
`publish-native-return status=1400`; the launcher observer recorded 0x42b.

release. The trace does not identify the failing publication substage or HWND.

concurrently after its mandatory root was copied; the investigation records
that distinct witness and its subsequent ownership correction. The Winmine
trace does not establish that same interleaving, and neither it nor the
similarly unlocalized SOL 1400 may be declared fixed on that basis alone.
