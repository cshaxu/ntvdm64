# DOSX SMARTDRV ABI and conditional lifecycle

For owned `dpmi/smartdrv.inc`, `dpmi/dxboot.asm`, `dpmi/486/dxboot.asm` and `dpmi/dxstrt.asm`, the companion `smartdrv-guest-abi-observer.py` records exact current and both-original hashes. All four are byte-identical to both originals; the 486 `dxstrt.asm` and guest makefile are cross-partition inputs read for context, with no disposition proposed here.

The original include defines SMARTDRV device name, cache flags and IOCTL read/write structures. In both boot sources, the call to `ShrinkSmartDrv` and its complete helper are guarded by `IFDEF OBSELETE` (spelling exactly as in source). If that macro is selected, the helper opens SMARTAAR via INT 21h, reads its status, computes cache shrink, records `SMRTDRVDelta` and issues IOCTL write; failed or short writes reset the recorded delta before closing. In the original exit code, `dxstrt.asm` has initialized zero delta and only attempts the matching growth/close path when the delta is nonzero. SMARTDRV is a guest device and DOS owns its guest handle/IOCTL; those declarations do not by themselves identify any host SMARTDRV provider or validate the shipped DOSX image provenance.

The original historical recipe and 486 link files are evidence of a possible build route, not proof of `OBSELETE` definition or of binary source correspondence. A literal source guard cannot establish which branches were assembled into the prebuilt shipped DOSX. No hash-bound real guest test with SMARTDRV shrink/grow, exit, short IOCTL, or failed open was identified in this pass; existing general DOSX boot witnesses cannot prove this optional branch. Keep these source rows pending for profile applicability and original capability evidence, rather than declaring either a defect or whole-feature exclusion. Per existing policy no guest binaries are modified or rebuilt. The lifecycle facts describe source, not confirmed runtime.
