# Legacy network BOPs and guest-facing recovery boundaries

This source-first group concerns the pending original `softpc.new/base/inc/novell.h`, `ipx.h`, and `tcpip.h` declarations and their distinct host profiles; it does not claim general networking is unavailable. No source, guest or product was changed.

The original BIOS table maps `NOVELL` to ODI driver BOPs 34h–3Dh with additional conditional 3Eh/3Fh, `NOVELL_IPX` to A0h–A2h, `NOVELL_TCPIP` to A4h–A5h, and its own DOS `WINSOCK` conditional to A6h. In the captured candidate, `bios-image-observations.json` contains relocation-verified targets for all 256 slots. `legacy-network-bop-observer.py` extracts a deterministic 20-slot subset and records source receipt and original candidate image hashes in `legacy-network-bop-evidence.json`: every slot 34h–3Fh and A0h–A7h points at `_illegal_bop` in that specific candidate. These are **not** freshly executed guest BOPs, do not establish today's installed image, and do not establish which immutable DOS application actually reaches the optional routes.

The Win16 Winsock `wow32/wsraw.c`/WSOCK32 path and DOS redirector/VDMREDIR NetBIOS or named-pipe paths are separate owners and cannot be used to claim ODI/IPX/LAN Workplace restoration. Conversely, disabled SoftPC BOPs do not prove those separate network services are broken. `shard-3/wfwnet-guest-input-role-review.md` notes a retained original WFWNET.DRV and its conditional `NWCS/NwcsInstalled` source path registering `VWIPXSPX.DLL`; that path and any external provider have not been dynamically bound. The inspected historical absence of the DLL in one system32 location is not proof of every loader search result or every WFW configuration.

## Finite original-first investigation and verification

1. Identify exact immutable guest binaries, configured DOS/Win16 entry points, and source lineage for any application or driver that would request ODI/IPX or LAN Workplace. Establish the original registration and unload owner and distinct BOP/VDD route; do not infer that a source file in the mirror or a WFWNET image alone invokes it.
2. For a demonstrated route, determine whether its original provider and required device/network backend are available in the product or can be adapted at a narrow host boundary. Preserve original ECB/TCB buffers, network address/socket identity, synchronous/error returns, asynchronous completion, interrupt and teardown sequencing. A UDP relay, dummy callback or blanket successful response is not automatically semantically equivalent; do not introduce it without a proved contract and registered exception.
3. Reproduce only with separately controlled peers and isolated test-owned package/data. Check discovery/open, one send/receive, failure/unsupported, async cancellation and guest task/worker teardown; verify bounds and immutable guest hashes. Explicitly report missing provider/device or guest input as a capability boundary, rather than treating other NetBIOS/Winsock tests as passes.
4. Bind the candidate/release image and exact BIOS table under test. If original route remains unreachable or an approved unsupported provider is selected, retain that evidence with the correct profile/capability boundary; do not claim current-profile source exclusion means the original network capability is fully recovered.

Existing RAP/DLC approvals apply only to their named VDMREDIR boundaries, not automatically to these separate IPX/ODI interfaces. This group remains pending; the slots above explain a concrete candidate-image limitation, not a newly reproduced customer defect.
