Source/header naming, map and object do not bind today's installed executable.

The NT4 console server's graphics resources are adapted by project `console_graphics.c` (ADAPTER-WIN32-058) and `console_compat.c` (ADAPTER-WIN32-048): the painter writes the adapted bitmap, invalidation is routed to the active graphics owner or text fallback, and worker-base publishes captured video. Original VGA/planar layout and dirty region selection remain original painter responsibilities; the adapter owns bitmap identity, capture/locking and publication lifecycle. The historical actual-object planar fixture (`planar-pixel-contract-review.md`) reports 26,114 cases/182,801 assertions with controlled DIB/invalidation; its explicit unsupported fixture boundaries and old image identity must remain attached.
