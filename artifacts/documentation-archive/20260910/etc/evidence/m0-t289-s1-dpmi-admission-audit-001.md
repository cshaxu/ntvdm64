artifact and build paths, plus 22 `dpmi32` host paths. DOSX assembly is a
guest image and cannot be linked into the host. The matching S1 ledger
partitions every host source group by its original owner.

No service may enter as an isolated trace response: descriptor/memory,
mode/stack and interrupt/fault groups depend on the same protected CPU frame,
LDT/IDT and exception producer; DOS translation additionally needs DEM and
COMMAND; VDD/debug retain later owner packages.

must inventory exact original imports and determine a minimal typed
`adapter-bochs` surface before any DPMI body, BOP ingress or CPU frame is
enabled. This preserves original interfaces without a second CPU executor or
