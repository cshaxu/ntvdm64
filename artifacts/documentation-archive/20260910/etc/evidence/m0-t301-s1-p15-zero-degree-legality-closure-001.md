## Method and controls

`tools/governance/export-t301-s1-zero-degree-legality.mjs` reads only the two
approved original MVDM roots and the frozen/rebased ledger inputs.  For every
definition identity it verifies its source hash, original relative path,
membership authority, linkage condition, parseable original body and
conditional-directive context.  Historic SoftPC macro-shaped forms such as
`IFNn` are recognised as definitions by their physical body rather than being
misclassified as missing modern C prototypes.

The primary row-level record is
`operations/mvdm-zero-degree-legality-ledger.tsv`.  The companion exception
ledger is intentionally header-only: no exception row survived the gates.

## Supersession

Earlier P11--P14 working counts relied on a line-offset implementation that
treated CRLF as one byte.  Their historical rows remain evidence, but their
aggregate counts are superseded by the regenerated inputs and this legality
closure.  No first-degree source body was selected or classified while making
this correction.
