## Explicit limitations and transfer

The foundation neither loads a guest image nor implements BOP, OpenNT,
SoftPC/CCPU, Win32 host services, firmware product boot or full device
composition. It transfers only typed opaque machine mechanics to the
canonical OpenNT source-supply and later adapter-recovery packages.
